About

The short version.

I'm from Temple Cloud, near Bristol. I've worked in the restaurant and pub sector locally since I was 15, which gave me an early grounding in working with people, keeping calm under pressure, and getting things done as part of a team — starting as a kitchen porter at The Lazy Lobster, then as a kitchen assistant at The Temple Inn.

After finishing my A-levels I took on a product research contract with Inspecs Ltd in Bath, helping develop a gaming eyewear brand — my first taste of working independently on a proper project. I'm now a First Line Engineer at Penelope Technical Ltd, and studying Creative Music Technology at UWE Bristol.

Outside of work, music is a big part of my life — I play a range of instruments, specialising in guitar, and perform at open mic nights around Bristol. It's a long-term ambition of mine to work in the music industry.
